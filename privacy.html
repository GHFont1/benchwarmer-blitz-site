Benchwarmer Blitz

Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Benchwarmer Blitz is an offline basketball arcade game published by Benjamin Scalzo. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking tools, in-app purchases, or third-party advertising SDKs.

The app saves best scores, unlocked balls, and settings locally on your device. These values are not sent to us. You can erase them using Settings → Reset scores and locker, or by deleting the app. Device backups may retain local app data according to your device settings.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. Optional haptics use the device's vibration feature. Sound effects are synthesized on the device.

Apple and Google may separately process store, installation, diagnostic, and purchase-account information according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from the game.

Support

Swipe the ball upward to shoot. A longer swipe adds power. You can also use the Shoot button and aim arrows. The Park, Wobble, and Moonball courts each save their own best score.

For support, email bennyscalzo@greatcallathletics.com. Include your device model, operating system version, and what happened. Any information you voluntarily email is used to answer your request; it is not collected by the game.
